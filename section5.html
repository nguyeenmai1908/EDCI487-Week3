Teaching Approaches

Four sparks. Not a lesson plan.

These aren't prescriptions — they're starting points. One of them will probably already feel like yours.

Start local

The tool you used today could work with students too. Their own block is the most powerful text.

Lead with story

Leandro didn't start with data. Neither did you. A person before a concept is almost always right.

Make space for why

"Why is our street hotter?" is a climate justice question. Students will ask it if you let them.

Show what changed

Injustice without agency crushes. Every hard truth needs at least one story of something that got better.

One thing before you go

Think about a grade level you're likely to teach. Pick one of the four sparks above — or your own. Describe one moment in a classroom: a question you'd ask, an activity you'd run, or something you'd show. Just one. Specific enough that you could actually do it.
