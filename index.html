Module Overview

Why justice is inseparable from climate?

Before you arrive in class this week, this module will ground you in the foundational concepts of climate justice — a framework that connects environmental harm to systems of race, class, gender, and colonial history.

As a future teacher, you'll leave with language and frameworks to bring these conversations into your own classroom — acknowledging complexity while remaining student-centred and trauma-informed.

Key Concepts

Brief introduction to this course's core ideas

5 concepts
Concept 01
Tree Equity
Fair distribution of urban trees and green space, with community voice in decisions.
Concept 02
Environmental Racism
Racialized communities disproportionately experience heat, pollution, and environmental risk.
Concept 03
Intersectionality
Climate vulnerability increases when identities and social conditions overlap.
Concept 04
Climate Apartheid
Wealth can buy protection while many communities face the worst impacts.
Concept 05
Environmental Colonialism
Colonial power patterns continue through land, resource, and environmental decisions.
